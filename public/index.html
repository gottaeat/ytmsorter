How to get your YouTube cookies
  1. Open YouTube in your browser, sign in, and choose your channel.
  2. Open Developer Tools → Network, then reload YouTube.
  3. Select a request to www.youtube.com. Under Request Headers, copy the complete Cookie value.
  4. Paste it below and choose Connect YouTube.

A Netscape cookies.txt export also works. These credentials give account access: paste them only into this local app.

Saved cookies are not encrypted by this app. Uncheck to keep them only for this tab. Backups never include cookies.
